## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io)**  

  Premier generative voice AI cloud platform offering Flash/Turbo low-latency TTS, instant and professional voice cloning, AI dubbing, sound effects, and conversational voice agents.



- **[PlayHT](https://play.ht)**  

  Cloud speech synthesis and voice cloning platform featuring real-time streaming APIs, emotional prosody controls, and multi-speaker podcast generation.



- **[Murf.ai](https://murf.ai)**  

  Cloud AI voiceover studio enabling creators and enterprises to produce studio-grade narration with pitch, pause, emphasis, and video-sync timeline controls.



- **[Resemble AI](https://resemble.ai)**  

  Enterprise generative voice platform combining real-time speech-to-speech voice conversion, neural TTS cloning, and PerTh perceptual audio watermarking.



- **[Speechify](https://speechify.com)**  

  Consumer and developer text-to-speech platform transforming documents, articles, and books into natural high-speed audio across web, mobile, and API.



- **[WellSaid Labs](https://wellsaidlabs.com)**  

  Enterprise AI voiceover studio delivering high-clarity voice avatars, custom phonetic dictionaries, and collaborative script production for corporate training.



- **[LOVO AI (Genny)](https://lovo.ai)**  

  Cloud AI voice generator and multimedia editor providing 500+ expressive voices across 100+ languages for marketing, e-learning, and video dubbing.



- **[Descript (Overdub & Underlord)](https://descript.com)**  

  AI-powered audio and video editor featuring Overdub voice cloning and text-based speech editing that lets creators type corrections directly into recorded voice tracks.



- **[Deepgram Aura](https://deepgram.com)**  

  Enterprise real-time voice AI API delivering ultra-low-latency streaming text-to-speech engineered specifically for high-throughput conversational voice agents.



- **[Cartesia (Sonic)](https://cartesia.ai)**  

  State-space model (SSM) voice synthesis cloud offering sub-90ms time-to-first-audio streaming, emotional control, and instant voice cloning.



- **[Hume AI (Octave TTS)](https://hume.ai)**  

  Empathic voice AI platform generating emotionally expressive speech and conversational voice agents driven by contextual tone and prosody understanding.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[VoiceStudio (debpalash/VoiceStudio)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source AI voice synthesis and speech studio project referenced in the seed list (`debpalash/VoiceStudio`) for local voice generation and audio workflows.



- **[Kokoro-82M & Kokoro-FastAPI (hexgrad/kokoro)](https://github.com)**  

  Apache 2.0 licensed, 82M-parameter open-weight TTS model delivering natural studio-quality speech faster than real time on CPU and GPU, with OpenAI-compatible API wrappers (`remsky/Kokoro-FastAPI`).



- **[F5-TTS (SWivid/F5-TTS)](https://github.com)**  

  Diffusion Transformer (DiT) and Flow-Matching open-source speech synthesis model offering high-fidelity zero-shot voice cloning, multi-speaker dialogue, and WebUI/CLI tools.



- **[CosyVoice & CosyVoice 2 (FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice)](https://github.com)**  

  Alibaba's Apache 2.0 open-source multilingual voice generation model supporting 3-second zero-shot voice cloning, cross-lingual synthesis, emotion instructions, and bi-directional streaming.



- **[Fish Speech (fishaudio/fish-speech)](https://github.com)**  

  Leading open-source dual-autoregressive Transformer TTS engine supporting zero-shot and few-shot voice cloning across English, Chinese, Japanese, and European languages without G2P dependencies.



- **[Chatterbox (resemble-ai/chatterbox)](https://github.com)**  

  MIT-licensed open-source TTS model from Resemble AI featuring zero-shot voice cloning, emotion exaggeration sliders, and built-in Perth perceptual watermarking.



- **[Orpheus TTS (canopyai/Orpheus-TTS)](https://github.com)**  

  Apache 2.0 open-source Llama-based speech language model producing expressive human speech with inline emotion tags (`<laugh>`, `<sigh>`, `<gasp>`) and ~100ms streaming latency.



- **[Dia-1.6B (nari-labs/dia)](https://github.com)**  

  Apache 2.0 open-weight 1.6B parameter dialogue-native TTS model generating realistic multi-speaker conversations and non-verbal audio cues directly from script tags.



- **[Sesame CSM-1B (SesameAILabs/csm)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source Conversational Speech Model generating context-aware, natural conversational prosody conditioned on preceding dialogue audio and text turns.



- **[Spark-TTS (SparkAudio/Spark-TTS)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source single-stream BiCodec speech synthesis framework built on Qwen2.5 enabling fine-grained voice attribute control (gender, pitch, speed) and zero-shot cloning.



- **[AllTalk TTS (erew123/alltalk_tts)](https://github.com)**  

  Full-featured open-source TTS studio and API server wrapping XTTS-v2, Piper, Parler-TTS, and VITS with a web UI, multi-voice narrator mode, and low-VRAM streaming.



- **[Coqui XTTS-v2 & Idiap Coqui-TTS (idiap/coqui-ai-TTS)](https://github.com)**  

  Community-maintained continuation of Coqui TTS providing XTTS-v2 cross-lingual zero-shot voice cloning across 17 languages with sub-200ms streaming latency.



- **[GPT-SoVITS (RVC-Boss/GPT-SoVITS)](https://github.com)**  

  Powerful open-source few-shot voice cloning and TTS web studio capable of cloning a voice from just 5 seconds of audio or fine-tuning high-accuracy custom voice models in minutes.



- **[OpenVoice & OpenVoice V2 (myshell-ai/OpenVoice)](https://github.com)**  

  MIT-licensed instant voice cloning library offering granular style control over emotion, accent, rhythm, pauses, and intonation across multiple languages.



- **[MeloTTS (myshell-ai/MeloTTS)](https://github.com)**  

  Lightweight, high-quality open-source multilingual TTS engine supporting English (American, British, Indian, Australian), Spanish, French, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean on CPU.



- **[Piper TTS (rhasspy/piper)](https://github.com)**  

  Fast, local, privacy-first open-source neural text-to-speech system powered by VITS and ONNX Runtime, ideal for Home Assistant, Raspberry Pi, and offline desktop apps.



- **[StyleTTS 2 (yl4579/StyleTTS2)](https://github.com)**  

  Foundational open-source style-diffusion TTS architecture achieving human-level naturalness and expressive zero-shot style transfer.



- **[Bark (suno-ai/bark)](https://github.com)**  

  Transformer-based open-source text-to-audio model by Suno generating multilingual speech alongside realistic laughter, sighs, music, and ambient sound effects.



- **[ChatTTS (2noise/ChatTTS)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source generative speech model tailored for conversational dialogue scenarios with fine-grained control over laughter, pauses, and prosody tokens.



- **[Parler-TTS (huggingface/parler-tts)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source text-to-speech model from Hugging Face that synthesizes speech guided by natural-language descriptions of speaker pitch, gender, speaking rate, and acoustics.



- **[OuteTTS (edwko/OuteTTS)](https://github.com)**  

  Pure language-modeling open-source TTS architecture supporting `llama.cpp` GGUF quantization for local CPU/GPU deployment and zero-shot speaker cloning.



- **[OpenedAI Speech (matatonic/openedai-speech)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source drop-in replacement for the OpenAI `/v1/audio/speech` API powered by Piper and XTTS-v2 for seamless integration into Open WebUI, LibreChat, and voice agents.



- **[Retrieval-based Voice Conversion — RVC (RVC-Project/Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI)](https://github.com)**  

  Industry-leading open-source real-time speech-to-speech voice conversion studio enabling low-latency singing and speaking voice transformation.
